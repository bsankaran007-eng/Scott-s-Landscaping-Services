## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, reliable, and premium identity for high-end residential landscaping and lawn care services in North Alabama. The aesthetic marries southern residential warmth with crisp, contemporary craft. It departs from commoditized utility lawn care by projecting the precision of estate management, backed by flawless service delivery and stellar reputation.

The design movement combines **Modern Tactile Minimalism** with **Refined Southern Organicism**:
- **Trust & Precision:** Clean, structured card containers, unambiguous information architecture, and immaculate typographic hierarchies that reflect meticulous horticultural care.
- **Physical Substance:** Tactile interaction cues, subtly debossed surfaces, and weighted interactive states that mirror deliberate, grounded outdoor labor.
- **Warm Southern Hospitality:** Sun-bleached limestone and warm sandy earth tones offset deep Appalachian forest greens, avoiding sterile software aesthetics in favor of welcoming curbside dignity.
- **Immediate Utility:** High-contrast contact signals, tap-friendly execution targets, and friction-free quoting workflows engineered for mobile users managing properties on the go.

## Colors

The color palette grounds the UI in authentic botanical deep tones, lively turf accents, and mineral earth foundations.

### Roles & Application
- **Primary (`#164E24` - Deep Forest Slate):** Represents heritage, density, and institutional trust. Used for dominant header surfaces, top-level navigation states, prominent brand markers, and primary call-to-action fills.
- **Secondary (`#2E7D32` - Lush Meadow):** Represents growth, vibrant hydration, and prime lawn health. Deployed for active status badges, positive verification pips, progress states, and secondary action highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#D4A373` - Sandstone Soil):** Represents pine straw, river rock, and tailored hardscaping. Acts as an accent for rating stars, service tier borders, seasonal notice backings, and prestige badges.
- **Neutral Core (`#1B241E` - Charcoal Spruce):** Dark organic off-black used for all high-contrast primary typography and icons, preventing the synthetic harshness of pure black.
- **Backgrounds & Surfaces:**
  - Base canvas: `#F9F8F6` (Limestone Wash) delivers a warm, glare-free foundation.
  - Card & Container surfaces: Pure `#FFFFFF` (Crisp White) for crisp separation over the limestone canvas.
  - Secondary containers: `#F4EBD9` (Soft Earth Cream) for highlighted quoting panels, testimonial cards, and guarantee callouts.

## Typography

The typographic system exclusively implements **Plus Jakarta Sans** to unify geometric clarity with modern, friendly warmth.

### Style Directives
- **Headlines:** Use dense letter-spacing (`-0.03em` on Display, `-0.02em` on Large) and tight leading to produce sturdy headline blocks reminiscent of editorial architecture journals.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Ratings (`5.0`), pricing tiers, and acre counters must use tabular or tight tracking with heavy weights (`700` or `800`) to accentuate trust and numerical precision.
- **Micro-Labels & Trust Markers:** `label-sm` utilizes uppercase rendering with positive tracking (`0.06em`) for categorical chips, license badges ("LICENSED & INSURED"), and geographical tags ("HUNTSVILLE & MADISON COUNTY").

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system that maintains structural cohesion from compact hand-held devices to ultrawide desktop monitors.

### Breakpoints & Grid Scaling
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4 columns. Gutter: `1rem`, outer margins: `1rem` (`margin`). Layout defaults to a single-column flow with full-width action strips pinned to the bottom viewport.
- **Tablet (640px – 1024px):** 8 columns. Gutter: `1.5rem` (`gutter`), outer margins: `2rem`. Two-column arrangement for service packages, 4-column distribution for service cards.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns. Gutter: `1.5rem`, outer canvas max-width: `1280px` centered with variable dynamic margin. Three- and four-column configurations for service grids and portfolio comparisons.

### Rhythmic Rules
- **Stacking:** Component internal spacing adheres strictly to 8-point base rhythm (`space-xs` through `space-xl`).
- **Section Pacing:** Main page sections rely on `space-xl` multiplied twofold (5rem) on desktop to guarantee uncluttered, premium white space.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages a hybrid model of **Tonal Layering** paired with **Sunlit Ambient Shadows**, mirroring clear outdoor daylight over structured surfaces.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Flat `#F9F8F6` background. No elevation.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Service Units):** Surface `#FFFFFF` layered over `#F9F8F6` with a delicate hairline boundary (`1px solid rgba(27, 36, 30, 0.08)`) and an ambient, warm downward drop: `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(22, 78, 36, 0.05), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(27, 36, 30, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Elements & Hover States):** Lifted components (e.g., interactive quote selectors, pricing tiers) elevate via: `box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -6px rgba(22, 78, 36, 0.10), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(27, 36, 30, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Action Bars & Modals):** Fixed instant-call mobile bars and full modals receive sharp structural separation with: `box-shadow: 0 20px 32px -8px rgba(27, 36, 30, 0.16)`.

Avoid high-contrast artificial drop shadows or thick cartoon borders. Transitions between levels must resolve within `150ms ease-out`.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a **Rounded** shape language (`roundedness: 2`).

### Corner Radius System
- **Base Components (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to form text inputs, small badge containers, calendar pickers, and nested control chips.
- **Containers & Cards (`1rem` / 16px):** Standard for property assessment cards, review carousels, service blocks, and booking forms.
- **Hero & Modal Sheets (`1.5rem` / 24px):** Utilized for bottom pull-up sheets on mobile devices and primary desktop modal windows.
- **Pill Elements (Full Round / 9999px):** Strictly reserved for dynamic call-to-action buttons ("Call Now", "Get Instant Quote"), rating chips, and status markers to ensure high tactile affordance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Pill-shaped, `#164E24` background with pure white text, paired with `label-lg` typography. Padding: `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Subtle pressed feedback effect: `transform: scale(0.98)`.
- **Secondary CTA:** Pill-shaped, transparent background with a 1.5px border of `#164E24`, `#164E24` text.
- **Emergency / Direct Phone Action:** Pill-shaped, vibrant `#2E7D32` background with white text, left-aligned phone handset icon, and glowing ping indicator for instant click-to-call.

### Contact Badges & Trust Seals
- **5.0 Google Rating Badge:** Encased in `#FFFFFF` with hairline `#D4A373` border; features five `#D4A373` star icons, bold "5.0" metric in `#1B241E`, and "Huntsville's Choice" micro-label.
- **Local Service Area Badge:** Soft `#F4EBD9` background, `#164E24` text, pin icon, fully rounded pill.

### Cards
- **Service Cards:** Pure white background, `1rem` radius, subtle Level 1 ambient shadow. Upper edge holds an optional 3px status bar in `#2E7D32` for popular service packages (e.g., "Full Turf Maintenance", "Seasonal Pine Straw & Mulch").
- **Before / After Comparison Cards:** Split interactive frame with tactile center slider bar in `#D4A373` with rounded drag handle.

### Input Fields
- Flat white fill, `0.5rem` radius, 1.5px border in `rgba(27, 36, 30, 0.15)`. Text in `body-md`. Focus state shifts border to `#164E24` with an outer ring of `3px solid rgba(22, 78, 36, 0.12)`.
- Specialized mobile inputs: Large numeric keypad triggers for lot size (acreage) and instant telephone formatting.

### Chips & Selectors
- **Service Add-ons (e.g., "Aeration", "Edging", "Leaf Cleanup"):** Toggle chips with `0.5rem` radius, unselected: `#FFFFFF` with light border; selected: `#164E24` background with `#FFFFFF` text and green checkmark icon.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Circular radio and rounded square (`4px` radius) checkboxes in `20px x 20px`. Active fill in `#164E24` with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` indicators.
